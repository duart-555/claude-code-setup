# Skills

`skills/` holds 154 skills. 150 are vendored under verified upstream MIT licenses (see
`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`). Update any group from its upstream when you want newer versions.

## Included without a confirmed license

Four skills are included because the maintainer of this repo chose to share their whole setup, but
their upstream publishes no license (or none was recorded). Treat them as all rights reserved by
their authors. If you fork this repo publicly, remove them or get permission first.

| Skill | Upstream |
|---|---|
| `apple-design-hig` | [dickwu/apple-design-skill](https://github.com/dickwu/apple-design-skill), no license published |
| `apple-design`, `agent-reach`, `humanizer` | upstream not recorded |

If you are an upstream author and want one of these removed, open an issue and it will be.

## Left out

Runtime-generated or personal: `learned`, `synced`.
